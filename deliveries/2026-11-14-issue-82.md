## Why

_From the PR description (author-written)._

Issue #82's last criterion is that the website lists the submission.

## How

_From the PR description (author-written)._

Same pattern as Days 3-5 (cdadd9e): the page entry, an exact-URL assertion in `check-site.sh`, and `check-links.sh`'s time budget. The link uses the repo form (`github.com/qodo-hackathon/software-factory-hackathon/discussions/8#…`) like Days 1-5, not the `orgs/` form in the issue, because `check-links.sh`'s comment lookup reads owner/repo from the URL. `check-links.sh`'s own comment said its budget was full at 16 jobs; Day 6 makes 18 (12 links, 6 comment lookups), so the cap goes from 8 to 10: batch 1 is 10 plain links (at most 66 s), batch 2 is 2 links plus 6 lookups (at most 198 s), worst case 264 s, 36 s inside the step's 5-minute timeout, with no workflow change.

- `scripts/check-site.sh`: asserts the Day 6 link on the built page.
- `scripts/check-links.sh`: raises the batch cap from 8 to 10.
