# feat: add a How a change ships walkthrough to the hackathon page

**PR:** [#147](https://github.com/lioramilbaum/lioramilbaum.github.io/pull/147) · **Opened:** 2026-11-24 · **Hackathon:** [Day 15: Show your factory](https://github.com/orgs/qodo-hackathon/discussions/17): this section is the full walkthrough that my Day 15 post links to.

## Why

_From the PR description (author-written)._

Issue #146: the hackathon page listed the daily submissions and linked to AGENTS.md and the delivery docs, but never said how a change moves through the factory. A visitor had to read a long rules file or fourteen daily posts to find out. Day 15 of the hackathon asks for a public walkthrough, and my LinkedIn post for it is a short teaser that needs the full story to link to.

## How

_From the PR description (author-written)._

- The walkthrough is a section of the page people already visit, with its own anchor, `#how-a-change-ships`, so a post can link straight to it.
- It is written for a visitor, in the first person: six steps from ticket to main, who acts at each, and the three where I decide. Then one example from this week, one lesson, and what doesn't work yet.
- The diagram is an image with alt text, and the numbered list under it is its text equivalent. It has its own dark panel, so it reads on the light and the dark page. No script, stylesheet or font is added (AGENTS.md rule 4).
- The image is 1280 px wide, so the docs layout gets one line that lets images shrink to the column. The homepage layout is untouched and the homepage build is byte-identical to main's, so no homepage screenshot is due (rule 7).
- The section adds two external links (PR #138 and issue #67), which takes the page to 36 link-check jobs: exactly the two-batch limit at the old cap of 18. The cap goes to 20, so the Day 15 and Day 16 links still fit in two batches, 264 s in the worst case, inside CI's 5-minute step timeout.

## What

_From the PR description (author-written)._

- `docs/qodo-software-factory-hackathon.md`: the "How a change ships" section, between the intro and "Follow along".
- `assets/images/how-a-change-ships.png`: the diagram, 1280×1600, 189 KB.
- `_layouts/docs.html`: `.content img { max-width: 100%; height: auto; }`. Without it the page scrolls sideways at 320 px, and `scripts/check-browser.sh` fails.
- `scripts/check-site.sh`: asserts the section on the built page: the heading and its anchor, the six step leads as list items in order, the sentence about the three decisions, the diagram before the list with its exact alt text and a 300 KB limit, the three sub-headings, at least three "doesn't work yet" items, and the links to #138 and #67.
- `scripts/check-links.sh`: batch cap 18 → 20, and the budget comment rewritten for 22 links and 14 lookups (36 jobs).

## Limitations

_From the PR description (author-written)._

- Red CI run (AGENTS.md rule 8): [run 45497005663](https://github.com/lioramilbaum/lioramilbaum.github.io/actions/runs/45497005663) on the deliberately broken revision ffe3ffc (this PR without the section in the page) failed at "Check site": `hackathon page: How a change ships section missing (#how-a-change-ships)`.
- On a phone the diagram is shown about 300 px wide and its text is too small to read. The numbered list under it carries the same content. CI checks that the page does not scroll sideways, not that the image is legible.
- The `qodo-findings` status is advisory: no ruleset on `main` requires it yet (AGENTS.md rule 9 lists the steps to create one). The section says so.
- Step 5 describes a workflow and a guard that are configured in Qodo's portal, not in this repo. No check here notices if they are changed or switched off.
- The section's facts (four pages, eleven rules, the example, the lesson) were checked by hand against the repo when it was written. CI asserts the section's structure, not that those facts stay true.
- `check-links.sh` is a non-blocking CI step that needs the network. Locally it ran all 36 checks, all 200. The Day 17 link will start a third batch, so the budget must change before it is added.
